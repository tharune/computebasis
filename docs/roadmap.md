# Scoped roadmap

Each task has a tested development slice and separate acceptance evidence. Public examples are invented and the project stays indicative/read-only/paper until the relevant operating gates are met.

| Task | Implemented slice | Next acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CB-01 Contracts | Immutable domain types, units, Decimal strings, extensions and generated schema/UI types | Durable migrations, end-to-end consumers and registered model ownership |
| CB-02 Market data | Read-only admission/transport; advertised-price and candidate offer/token parsers | Vendor rights/accounts, certified exact units, firm physical identity/terms and observable source SLAs |
| CB-03 Benchmark | Exact cohorts, known quantity conversion, deduplication, bounded weights and diagnostics | Empirical source quality/support policy and independent uncertainty validation |
| CB-04 Procurement economics | Explicit denominators/cost ownership, inverse bid/ask, full-node allocation and tick bounds | Approved real collection, performance, cost and stress inputs with model validation |
| CB-05 Treasury | Full dates, advances/prepaids/reserves/facilities and cash-limited purchase ticks | Signed terms, common-account baseline and cleared-statement reconciliation |
| CB-06 Workloads | Exact measured setup, SLO/memory/parallel goodput and complete allocated GPU costs | Permitted benchmark jobs, quality/privacy, buyer demand and actual token/billing meters |
| CB-07 Calibration and risk | Research holdouts, explicit exposures/loss ownership and supplied tail distributions | Representative empirical calibration, dependence/regime uncertainty and constrained allocation optimization |
| CB-08 Capacity and control | Physical interval checks, version-bound paper decisions and ambiguity holds | Transactional reservations, production identity/roles, balanced journal, outbox and recovery |
| CB-09 Contracts and capital | Forward readiness, receivable eligibility/unique pledges and waterfalls | Enforceable rights, verified lender eligibility/lockbox/servicing and funded obligations |
| CB-10 Product and reporting | Authenticated local API and hashed development snapshot replay | Explainable user workbench, actuals/break reports, event delivery and retention controls |
| CB-11 Operating certification | Documented per-component gates and offline checks | Independent security/model/legal/finance review, incident coverage, restoration and measured load drills |
| CB-12 Execution extensions | Scope and evidence boundaries | Actually available instruments/accounts, approved custody/authority, funded reserves and durable per-rail reconciliation |

Build in dependency order: certified observations and terms; qualified performance/meters; validated factors; durable shared capacity/treasury/control; witnessed operating evidence; then each authorized execution rail. Financing and hedge research cannot repair an unverified underlying cash flow.
