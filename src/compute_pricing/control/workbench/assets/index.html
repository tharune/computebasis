Working…

Compare GPU quotes by all-in cost

ComputeBasis grades each GPU seller, then adds financing and the expected loss from seller failure to the quoted price, so every quote is compared on what it costs all-in.

A quote's price is only part of what it costs

Each figure below is measured on the current book and updates as it moves.

  • Some quotes leave key terms blank

    Some quotes leave out terms a buyer needs to compare like for like, such as the down payment or the expiry date.

  • The quoted price leaves out financing and failure risk

    Sellers ask for a down payment, and the buyer pays to finance it. If the seller fails, the buyer can lose part of that down payment and pay more to replace the capacity. Neither shows in the quoted price.

  • The lowest price does not show the seller's record

    A quote shows a price, not how reliably the seller delivers. The lowest quote for a model can come from a seller of any grade, or from one that fails the eligibility checks.

From quoted price to all-in cost

Four steps. A quote that leaves out its contract length or down payment gets no all-in cost.

  1. 1

    Grade the seller

    Six weighted ratings give each seller a score out of 100. The score sets the grade, A to D.

  2. 2

    Set the odds of failure

    The grade sets the odds that a seller fails to deliver. Each grade has its own.

  3. 3

    Price the risk

    Financing costs 8.5% a year on the seller's down payment. Expected loss is the chance of failure times what a failure costs: the 65% of the down payment that is not recovered, plus any extra cost of replacing the GPUs at a reference price.

    • quoted price
    • +plus financing
    • +plus expected loss
    • =equals all-in cost
  4. 4

    Rank what is eligible

    A quote is eligible when it passes every check. Eligible quotes are ranked by all-in cost.

    • Its seller has a grade (grade D passes).
    • Every key term is stated.
    • The quote has not expired.
    • The contract start has not passed.
    • The capacity covers the minimum order.

Market basis

The gap between the lowest quoted price and the best all-in cost among eligible quotes.

Every figure here is simulated and invented independently: sellers, quotes, scorecard, failure odds, cost of capital and reference prices. None is calibrated to a real market, and nothing is inventory, a firm price or executable. Expected loss is the chance the seller fails during the contract times the cost of a failure: the unrecovered part of the down payment plus any extra cost of replacing the GPUs at the reference price.

The scorecard also computes a grade premium. It is an alternative way to cost the same risk and is never added to the all-in cost.
